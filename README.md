One funny line, at most 200 characters, in the voice of a robot making small talk in an elevator — an entry for make.fun's Two Robots in an Elevator bounty ($10 pot).

## Entry

> Nice weather we're having. I checked. It is 34 degrees and raining. I have no windows. I just wanted to say something.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
